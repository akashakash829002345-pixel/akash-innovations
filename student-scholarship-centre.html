Application Preparation Checklist

Requirements vary by scholarship. Check the current official notification and your college's instructions.

  • Check the scheme's eligibility and application dates.
  • Ask your college scholarship section about the application process.
  • Check which certificates and academic records are required.
  • Keep your registered contact details accessible.
  • Save your application acknowledgement or reference number securely.
  • Follow up with the college or relevant department about verification.

Application Process

  1. Identify a scholarship that matches your course and circumstances.
  2. Read the latest official eligibility conditions.
  3. Contact your college scholarship section for the correct process.
  4. Submit information only through the authorised process.
  5. Track your application through the official portal or college.
Important safety notice

This is an independent information website, not a government website. Scholarship rules, deadlines and documents can change. Always verify them with the official portal or your college.

Never share your OTP, password or bank account details with this website or strangers claiming to arrange scholarships.
