Before You Apply

  1. Read the latest official scholarship notification.
  2. Confirm the eligibility rules and application dates.
  3. Ask your college scholarship section which documents are needed.
  4. Check whether documents must be original, scanned or verified.
  5. Keep your application acknowledgement safely.
Important: This is a general preparation checklist, not an official document list. Not every item is required for every scholarship. Confirm the exact requirements with the official portal or your college. Do not upload personal documents to this website.
