About Us

AKASH INNOVATIONS is an independent technology initiative founded by Akash Kummari in Andhra Pradesh, India. We aim to make technology easier to understand and useful for everyone through practical digital solutions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to use technology to simplify everyday challenges, improve access to public-service information and create meaningful impact in communities across India.
