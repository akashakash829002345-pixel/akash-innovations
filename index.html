About AKASH INNOVATIONS

AKASH INNOVATIONS is an independent technology initiative founded by Akash Kummari in Andhra Pradesh, India. We aim to make technology easier to understand and useful for everyone through practical digital solutions, accessible information and innovation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to use technology to simplify everyday challenges, improve access to public-service information and create meaningful impact in communities across India.

Our Vision

Building useful technology and making digital information more accessible to everyone.
