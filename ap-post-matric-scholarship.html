Andhra Pradesh Post-Matric Scholarship

RTF and MTF scholarship information

1. What is this scheme?

This scholarship supports eligible students studying after Class 10 in approved educational institutions.

2. Types of benefits

  • RTF: Reimbursement of eligible fees.
  • MTF: Maintenance assistance for eligible students.

Amounts depend on the course and current government rules. Verify the latest amounts on the official portal.

3. Eligible categories

The official portal lists SC, ST, BC, EBC, Kapu, Minority and Differently Abled categories. Conditions apply.

4. Important eligibility conditions

  • Eligible course and recognised institution.
  • Attendance requirements may apply.
  • Family income and other restrictions may apply.

5. How to apply

  1. Visit the official Jnanabhumi portal.
  2. Contact your college scholarship section.
  3. Confirm the current application process and required documents.
  4. Submit your application through the official process.
