Andhra Pradesh Post-Matric Scholarship

AP Post-Matric Scholarships – RTF and MTF

1. What is this scheme?

Post-Matric Scholarship supports eligible students studying after Class 10 in approved educational institutions.

పదవ తరగతి తర్వాత చదువుతున్న అర్హులైన విద్యార్థులకు ఈ పథకం ఆర్థిక సహాయం అందిస్తుంది.

2. Types of benefits

  • RTF: Reimbursement of eligible fees.
  • MTF: Maintenance assistance for eligible students.

Official portal information lists annual MTF amounts as: ITI ₹10,000, Polytechnic ₹15,000 and Degree and above ₹20,000. Amounts and applicable conditions must be confirmed for the current academic year.

3. Eligible categories

The official portal lists SC, ST, BC, EBC (other than Kapu), Kapu, Minority and Differently Abled categories.

Eligibility depends on the applicable government rules, course, institution and other conditions.

4. Important eligibility conditions

  • Eligible course and recognised institution.
  • Attendance requirement may apply.
  • Family income and other restrictions may apply.
  • Fresh and renewal application procedures may differ.

Check the current official guidelines before applying.

5. How to apply

  1. Visit the official Jnanabhumi portal.
  2. Contact your college scholarship section.
  3. Ask about the fresh or renewal application process.
  4. Submit the required information through the official process.
  5. Check your application status through official channels.
