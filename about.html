Who We Are

AKASH INNOVATIONS is an independent technology initiative founded by Akash Kummari in Andhra Pradesh, India. We explore practical ways to use technology to make digital information and useful services easier to access.

Our Mission

To make technology and reliable digital information more accessible to everyone through simple, useful and inclusive digital solutions.

Our Vision

To contribute to a digitally empowered India where people can discover information and use technology with greater confidence.

Our Objectives

  • Make public-service information easier to find.
  • Help students discover scholarship information.
  • Promote digital awareness and technology learning.
  • Build practical solutions for everyday challenges.
  • Encourage responsible and inclusive innovation.

Our First Initiative

Public Service India is our initial initiative. It provides organised information about public services, scholarships, government schemes and official resources.

Explore Public Service India

Our Independence

AKASH INNOVATIONS is an independent initiative. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any government department.
