🇮🇳 About Public Service India

Public Service India is an independent digital information platform developed by AKASH INNOVATIONS.

Our goal is to make government-service information easier to discover, understand and access.

🎯 Our Mission

To make useful public-service information accessible to everyone through simple, reliable and bilingual technology.

🚀 Our Vision

To build accessible digital solutions that create a positive impact in communities across India.

💡 Our Values

  • Technology for Everyone
  • Transparency and accuracy
  • Accessibility for all
  • Public awareness and digital inclusion

Brand: AKASH INNOVATIONS
